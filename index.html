About Solatech

Your Complete Technology & Branding Partner

Solatech Computers Private Limited is a Zimbabwean technology, printing and branding company located at Shop 7, Manica Chambers, Main Street, Mutare. We provide practical and reliable technology solutions to individuals, businesses, organisations and institutions.

With three branches in Mutare, our operations cover computer sales and repairs, networking, CCTV solutions, technology sales, digital printing, graphic design, branding and large-format printing.
