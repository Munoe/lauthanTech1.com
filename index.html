About Solatech

Technology & Creative Solutions Built For You.

Solatech Computers Private Limited is a Zimbabwean technology, printing and branding company based in Shop 7, Manica Chambers, Main Street, Mutare.

With three branches operating in Mutare, Solatech provides a broad range of services covering computer sales and repairs, networking, CCTV, technology sales, printing, graphic design, DTF branding and large-format printing.

We serve individuals, businesses, schools, churches, organisations, events and corporate clients with practical, reliable and creative solutions.
