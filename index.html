About Solatech

Technology, Printing & Branding Under One Roof.

Solatech Computers Private Limited is a Zimbabwean technology and printing company based in Mutare. We provide a wide range of technology products and professional services designed to help individuals, companies, schools, organisations and institutions work smarter.

Our operations are spread across three branches in Mutare, with two branches focused on computer technology, networking, repairs and technology sales, while our specialist branding branch focuses on customised branding and large-format printing.
