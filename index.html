Who We Are

Technology, Printing & Branding Under One Roof.

Solatech Computers Private Limited is a Zimbabwean technology, printing and branding company based in Mutare.

We provide complete technology solutions for individuals, companies, schools, organisations and institutions. Our services range from computer sales and repairs to networking, CCTV, professional printing and creative branding.

Solatech operates through three branches in Mutare. Two branches focus primarily on technology sales, computer repairs, networking and CCTV, while our specialist branding branch provides DTF printing, promotional products and large-format printing.
